# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are Thai-speaking business analysts and operators reviewing customer
feedback. Secondary users are recruiters and engineering reviewers evaluating the
project as an end-to-end NLP portfolio artifact.

## Product Purpose

Thai Review Intelligence turns Thai customer reviews into sentiment predictions,
confidence scores, batch summaries, and actionable issue signals. Success means a
user can analyze one review immediately, process a CSV of up to 100 reviews, and
understand the resulting sentiment mix without reading model internals.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, locally fluent.

## Anti-references

- Generic purple-gradient SaaS dashboards.
- Neon, dark-mode machine-learning control rooms.
- Card-heavy bento layouts that hide the main task.
- Decorative AI imagery, chat metaphors, or fake automation claims.

## Design Principles

1. Put the review and its result before analytics or decoration.
2. Use Thai language as first-class product content, not translated filler.
3. Separate model evidence from business interpretation.
4. Make uncertainty visible through confidence and probability detail.
5. Keep workflows familiar enough that the interface disappears into the task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic status
text in addition to color, reduced-motion support, 44px touch targets, and layouts
that remain usable at 200% zoom.
